Where do the next-token scores come from?

An open model running in this tab on WebGPU — Gemma 3 270M, SmolLM2, Qwen2.5, GPT-2. Weights download once from Hugging Face and then run on your GPU. Every score you see is the model's own logit over its whole vocabulary, and the KV cache switch controls the model's actual cache.
